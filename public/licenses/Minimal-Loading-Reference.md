# Minimal numerical loading screen

Visual reference: Lusion, https://lusion.co/ . Its live black preloader and large lower-left white three-digit counter were inspected on 25 October 2026. At a 1363×936 viewport, the digit wrapper used Aeonik 112px with 84px line height, at left:0/bottom:0. The inspected session remained at 000; complete counter motion and exit were not observed.

Mani requested a thin filling bar in addition to that number. This portfolio uses original DOM/CSS/TypeScript, a stable system sans-serif instead of Lusion's font files, modest safe-area gutters and a two-pixel bottom progress line. No Lusion assets, branding, WebGL scenes, font files or source bundle are copied. This is an adaptation of the minimal loading composition, not a claim of pixel-identical reproduction or Lusion endorsement.

The percentage represents completed preparation milestones from the portfolio's existing boot owner; it is not a byte-download or remaining-time estimate. The number reaches 100 after the real prepared target draw. Both the terminal/archive and compilation-stream loader directions are retired.
