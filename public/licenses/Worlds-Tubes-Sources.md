This portfolio uses an **independently authored implementation** of rounded,
sine-tapered moving tube geometry, parallel-transport section frames, a bounded
fixed-step follower, vertex pigment history, local analytic surface lighting and
a circular idle target. The original bundle was run locally as a research
reference; none of its engine, geometry/follower/shader implementation, textures,
demo code or postprocessing is included in the application. Existing Three.js
dependencies and their notices remain unchanged.

Adaptations to the observed design: nine restrained strands; exact displayed
smoke pigment; local shaded bodies in both themes; a 22 CSS-pixel, 12-second idle
circle at the last actual in-page pointer; one existing renderer/frame loop;
preserved native input, smoke and accessibility behavior. The reference is
credited as the design source, not represented as a copied component or a
pixel-identical reproduction. The creator's differing notices remain an open
question for any future distribution of their actual implementation; this
record neither relicenses the portfolio nor settles commercial-use rights.
