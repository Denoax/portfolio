# Galaxy Art Direction

Use for every new chapter composition and refinement pass.

1. Write the frame's single focal idea before styling it: subject, dominant mass, negative-space region, and one accent family.
2. Block the frame in grayscale. Require true black voids, an asymmetrical silhouette, and readable hierarchy before adding color or effects.
3. Coordinate the 3D focal mass and DOM typography as one poster. Keep metadata distant from display type; avoid centered title/subtitle/CTA arrangements.
4. Add light selectively: muted off-white for most particles, ion blue for energetic state, amber for a specific event, violet only as a rare secondary note.
5. Reject uniform point fields, universal glow, card layouts, dashboard chrome, decorative tech logos, and effects that do not strengthen the narrative.
6. Capture the intended viewport and judge the still image before judging animation. Refine until the eye lands immediately on the intended subject and the image remains distinctly Mani's.

Reference principles only: Galaxy Interactive for black space and confidence, Equinox for atmosphere, Portal for precision, and Lax Space for restraint. Never copy their assets or compositions.
