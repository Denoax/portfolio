# Portfolio Content

Use whenever adding, condensing, reordering, or presenting factual portfolio content.

1. Use only facts in the repository or explicit user directive. Never infer employers, dates, titles, outcomes, links, awards, technologies, or metrics.
2. Keep primary scene copy terse: identity, organization, period, role/state, and a few exact technical concepts. Put detailed facts in semantic secondary copy that remains accessible without precision interaction.
3. Translate structure into the gravitational narrative: AAFC periods become three anchors; infrastructure concepts become routed streams; ICPC becomes an optimal-path graph; the dual degree becomes intersecting fields; interests become quiet distant signals.
4. Preserve the exact meaning of dates, placements, locations, and achievements. Do not exaggerate scope or ownership.
5. Keep email, LinkedIn, and any available resume action functional and keyboard accessible. Do not expose phone or street address.
6. Reject cliché introductions, marketing claims, card grids, skill percentages, logos, and invented project copy.

The experience may reveal progressively, but a visitor must be able to understand the major professional and educational facts by ordinary scrolling.
